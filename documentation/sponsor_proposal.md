# SURAKSHA-NET: Technology Sponsorship & Seed Funding Proposal

**Proposal Title**: SURAKSHA-NET: Smart Soldier Protection, Survival & Automated Rescue System  
**Organization / Startup**: SURAKSHA Defense Technologies (Early-Stage Deep-Tech Venture)  
**Target Program**: Defense Innovation Organization (iDEX) / Technology Development Fund (DRDO-TDF) / Corporate CSR & Venture Grants  
**Project Lead**: Senior Systems & Embedded IoT Engineering Lead  
**Proposal Status**: Phase 1 Software Proof-of-Concept Complete; Seeking Phase 2 Hardware Seed Grant  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
SURAKSHA-NET is a simulation-validated, decentralized tactical telemetry and automated casualty triage system designed to safeguard infantry soldiers and frontline first responders operating in contested, non-networked environments.

Having achieved **100% completion of our zero-cost software architecture**—including cycle-accurate Wokwi circuit models, dual-core ESP32 C++ firmware, an offline Streamlit tactical command dashboard, and an automated regression test suite—we are seeking a **seed development grant of ₹5,00,000 (Five Lakh INR)** to transition our verified software proof-of-concept into a ruggedized, benchtop-integrated hardware prototype.

## 4. Key Milestones & Deliverable Schedule

- **Month 1 (Milestone 1)**: Completion of physical breadboard benchtop integration; verified transmission of JSON telemetry frames over physical 866 MHz LoRa link.
- **Month 2 (Milestone 2)**: Ground-to-ground line-of-sight RF range testing reaching $\ge 2.5\text{ km}$ across undulating rural terrain.
- **Month 3 (Milestone 3)**: Fabrication of first-generation custom SMT PCB wearable units and integration with cold-resistant LiFePO4 battery packs.
- **Month 4–5 (Milestone 4)**: Environmental testing across $-15^\circ\text{C}$ to $+50^\circ\text{C}$; IP67 water immersion validation.
- **Month 6 (Milestone 5)**: Live multi-node demonstration for military advisors and defense innovation grant committees featuring 3 wearables and 2 autonomous repeaters.
